# PythaLab

**Research lab at Yildiz Technical University (YTU), Istanbul — learning-based control, UAV systems,
mechatronics and rigorous AI research.**

PythaLab turns theory into algorithms, algorithms into reliable software, software into simulations
and experiments, and findings into academic outputs. Claims are tested before they are believed:
reproducible code, fair baselines, and honest reporting of what works and what does not.

## Research themes

**Learning-based flight control for UAVs**
Reinforcement learning grounded in optimal control (HJB/Bellman), meta-learning and residual policies
for fixed-wing and VTOL aircraft; online parameter estimation, actuator dynamics, data-driven
aerodynamic surrogates, 6-DOF digital twins, and validation from simulation through SIL/HIL to flight.

**Safe and nonlinear control**
Safety filters and shields that preserve a baseline autopilot, approximate dynamic programming,
model predictive control, adaptive and robust nonlinear control, with Lyapunov-based (ISS/UUB) analysis.

**Estimation and embedded systems**
Kalman filtering and sensor fusion, real-time control on microcontrollers, mechatronic test rigs
and actuator systems.

**Rigorous evaluation of AI systems**
Preregistered and placebo-controlled studies of LLM coding agents and small local models:
falsification-based self-repair, memory and abstention for coding agents, retrieval, and
risk-controlled scientific claim checking.

**AI research infrastructure**
Local-first research workspaces, multi-agent workflows for software and science, and literature
pipelines that turn papers into searchable, structured knowledge.

**Physics-informed generative modelling**
Generative models constrained by physics, with applications such as biosignal synthesis for
prosthetic control.

## Teaching

Undergraduate and graduate courses in Python programming, artificial intelligence, reinforcement
learning for control, applied reinforcement learning for mechatronic systems, capstone design, and
large language models — supported by browser-based interactive laboratories where students run
simulations and train policies without installing anything.

## Students

The lab supervises MSc theses and undergraduate researchers. Each student works in a private,
supervised repository with a common structure; progress is tracked through weekly reports,
assignments with evidence-based solutions, and written reviews.
